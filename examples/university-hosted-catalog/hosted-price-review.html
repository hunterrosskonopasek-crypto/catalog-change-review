CSV headers and comparison rules

Use UTF-8 CSVs. The two catalogs require sku,selling_unit,pack_qty,price_usd. Price terms require sku,source,order_unit,each_per_order_unit,agreed_price_usd. Additional columns are allowed. The source is the applicable agreement or amendment reference.

SKU matching preserves case, spaces and leading zeros. Use the same unit codes across files. Enter positive whole pack counts and nonnegative plain decimal prices with up to six decimal places. Missing keys, duplicate keys and missing evidence require review. A terms match covers only the fields shown. Confirm the agreement version and effective date yourself.
