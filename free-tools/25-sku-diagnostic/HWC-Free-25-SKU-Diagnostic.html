Which columns can I include?

Include a SKU column. After loading, use “Match optional columns” to choose the barcode, selling unit, specification link and part-number fields from your export. The template uses gtin, uom, spec_url, card_mpn and destination_mpn. Use the last two together for the part number displayed on a related-item card and the part number at its link destination. A blank pair means this row has no card comparison.

Match optional columns

Choose the column your export uses for each field. Leave a field on “Skip this field” if your file omits it. The card comparison needs both part numbers.
